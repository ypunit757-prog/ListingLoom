How it runs

An open-weight model does the writing
The server sends your notes to a model such as Gemma through a standard chat endpoint. Run it yourself with Ollama, or point it at any provider that hosts open models. Switching models is a change to two settings.
Your numbers stay on your server
Cost of goods, supplier names and unreleased designs are never sent to a closed AI company. Prices are calculated on the server from the fees for the marketplace you pick.
No per-listing bill
With your own model there is no token meter running. Writing the thousandth listing costs the same as the first, which is what makes a nine dollar plan workable.
